FAQ

Questions we hear first.

I’m not technical. Can I still work with you?+

Yes. We explain options in plain language, agree priorities with you and show working software every week.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?+

Yes — natively, or with one cross-platform codebase, depending on what your product needs.

Can you work with our existing engineering team?+

Yes. We can own a whole product, take on one layer such as cloud or mobile, or work embedded in your team.

What happens after launch?+

We monitor and support what we build, or hand it over with documentation and runbooks your team can own.
