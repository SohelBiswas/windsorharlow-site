FAQ

Questions about Cloud & DevOps.

Do we need Kubernetes?+

Not always. For many teams a managed runtime is simpler and cheaper. We'll recommend what fits your scale and your team.

Can you reduce our cloud costs?+

We start with a cost review, then right-size resources and remove waste. You'll see the numbers before and after.

Can you set this up for our team to run?+

Yes. Everything is in code, with documentation and runbooks, so your team can own it.
