FAQ

Questions about Salesforce.

We already have Salesforce. Can you improve it?+

Yes. We start with a short review of your org — data, automation and how people actually use it — and fix the biggest problems first.

Do you use configuration or code?+

Both. We use standard configuration and Flow wherever we can, and write Apex or Lightning components only where it is clearly better.

Can Salesforce talk to our other systems?+

Yes. We connect it to your ERP, website, billing and support tools so nobody has to copy data by hand.
