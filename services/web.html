FAQ

Questions about Web products.

Can our team update the site ourselves?+

Yes. We'll set up a content workflow that fits — a headless CMS or simple content files — and show your team how to use it.

Do you test for accessibility?+

Yes. Automated checks run with every release and we review key journeys manually; automated tools alone can't catch everything.

Can you work from our existing designs?+

Yes. We can build from your designs, extend your design system, or design it with you.
