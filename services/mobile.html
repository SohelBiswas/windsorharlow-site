FAQ

Questions about Mobile apps.

Should we build native or cross-platform?+

It depends on the features and the team who will maintain it. We'll recommend one in the first week, with the trade-offs written down — not a default.

Can you take over an app someone else built?+

Yes. We start with a short review of the code, release process and crash reports, then agree a plan to stabilise it before adding features.

Do you handle App Store and Google Play releases?+

Yes — signing, store listings, review submissions and a repeatable release pipeline so future updates are routine.
