FAQ

Questions about Backend systems.

Can you work with our existing backend?+

Yes. We usually start by mapping what exists, then improve it in safe steps rather than rewriting everything.

How do you handle security?+

Access control, encryption and secrets management are part of the design, and reviewed before each release.

Will we be locked in to you?+

No. Everything is documented, in your repositories and cloud accounts, with runbooks for your team.
