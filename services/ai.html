The AI pilot

Four weeks to a clear yes or no.

Every AI project starts the same way: one task, your documents, a test set you own, and a decision at the end.

Week 1

Pick and prepare

Agree on one task, gather the documents and write 30 to 50 real test questions with good answers.

Week 2

Build a working prototype

Retrieval over your documents and answers with sources, running in your own cloud account.

Week 3

Measure and harden

Run the test set, fix the failures, and add guardrails for refusals, privacy and cost.

Week 4

Real users, real decision

Put it in front of a small group, review the results, then scale it, change it or stop.

What you keep

Proof, not a demo.

  • The prototype code, in your repository
  • A test set you own and can rerun
  • Measured accuracy and cost per answer
  • A plan for production, or a clear reason to stop

FAQ

Questions about AI & ML.

Where should we start with AI?+

With one task that costs your team time today. We test it on your real data in a short prototype before you commit to a full build.

Is our data safe?+

We design for it: data stays in your accounts where possible, access is limited, and we choose providers and settings that don't train on your data.

How do you know the AI is working?+

We build a test set from real examples and check against it with every change, so quality is measured rather than guessed.
